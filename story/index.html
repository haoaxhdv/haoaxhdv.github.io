02The Culture Continues

Hyphy didn't end.
It got a label.

  1. 01Too $hortMoved to Oakland in 1980. Built West Coast rap from the ground up.
  2. 02E-40Vallejo. Founded Sick Wid It Records in 1989.
  3. 03Mac DreFounded Thizz Entertainment. Gone in 2004; still everywhere.
  4. 04Keak da SneakRaised in Oakland. Credited with coining “hyphy.”
  5. 05Mistah F.A.B.Signed to Thizz in 2004. “Ghost Ride It,” the Thug Tears series. Then he built Dope Era.

Still on the road

The Motion Detected Tour with Philthy Rich ran up and down Northern California this fall: Salinas, Fresno, Santa Cruz, San Jose, Vallejo, Chico, Santa Rosa, Sacramento, Oakland.
